# Recurring mistakes

Mistakes that have happened more than once. The AI reads the list at the start of every chat.
If you say "that mistake again", the AI writes it in here and suggests a lock.

"Locked by" shows how strongly the mistake is prevented: a hook or a test is strong; "text rule" or "no" is weak and should become a lock.
